About Our Organisation

Name: iQamata LiKhaya Non-Profit Organisation

Brief History: iQamata LiKhaya is a community-based NPO operating in South Africa that focuses on youth empowerment, community support, and social upliftment initiatives.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To build a community where every individual thrives in safety and support, fostering a culture of resilience, holistic wellness, and generational growth.

Mission: To promote responsible choices around alcohol, empower our youth through education and practical skills, and nurture a supportive environment for all through initiatives that strive to create lasting change, active community participation, and collaborative partnerships.
